# AC EVO Modding

Welcome to the **Assetto Corsa EVO** modding pipeline documentation.

AC EVO introduces a brand-new editor and SDK, representing a significant leap from AC1. This section covers the complete workflow for car creation, physics tuning, audio production, and driver animation.

## Overview

### 🚗 Car Modding

From FBX export specifications and bone structure to editor usage and final car configuration.

- **[Editor & FBX](/en/evo/car/)** — Editor workspace setup, FBX export specs, bone hierarchy, material system
- **[Physics](/en/evo/car/physics/)** — Car physics tuning pipeline
- **[Audio](/en/evo/car/audio/)** — FMOD audio production pipeline

### 🧍 Driver Animation

Create driver animations in Blender and import them into the editor.

- **[Animation Pipeline](/en/evo/driver/)** — Blender scene setup, animation specs, export & import

## Important Notes

> AC EVO is currently in **Early Access**. Modding tools may change with game updates. Kunos Simulazioni intends to maintain backwards compatibility, but future game updates may require maintenance of mod assets.
